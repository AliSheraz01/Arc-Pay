EasyZpay is a USDC payment application built on **Arc Network**, Circle's EVM-compatible Layer 1 blockchain. Instead of copying and pasting 42-character `0x` wallet addresses, you can send and receive USDC using a human-readable `@username` on the web or through our Telegram bot.

## Who EasyZpay is for

* **Everyday senders and receivers** who want fast, simple USDC transfers by `@username` instead of hex addresses.
* **Telegram users** who want to check balances, view history, and send USDC directly inside chat.
* **Teams and contributors** who want on-chain USDC bounties with escrow payouts or cross-chain USDC bridging via Circle CCTP.

## Three key benefits

1. **Pay by `@username`:** Register a free on-chain username once and share it anywhere, or send directly to someone else's `@username`.
2. **Fast settlement in USDC:** Arc Network uses USDC as its native gas token and settles transactions with deterministic finality in under one second.
3. **Web app + Telegram bot:** Use the full web app at `easyzpay.xyz` for sending, receiving, cross-chain bridging, and bounties, or pair your account with `@EasyzpayBot` for quick transfers in Telegram.

> [!NOTE]
> EasyZpay is an interface for interacting with smart contracts on Arc Network and Circle's CCTP bridge. Always double-check the recipient and network before confirming any transaction.
