Follow these steps to set up EasyZpay and send your first USDC payment on Arc Mainnet.

## 1. Open the app and connect your wallet

1. Go to [easyzpay.xyz](https://easyzpay.xyz) and click **Launch App**.
2. Click **Connect Wallet** in the top-right corner (or in the sidebar).
3. Sign in with your Web3 wallet (such as MetaMask or Coinbase Wallet) or Privy login.

## 2. Switch to Arc Mainnet

If your wallet is on another network, EasyZpay prompts you to switch to **Arc Mainnet** (Chain ID `5042`). You can also open the **Network** switcher in the sidebar and choose **Arc Mainnet**.

If your wallet does not have Arc Mainnet yet, see [Network details](/docs/network-details) for the RPC URL and chain parameters.

## 3. Get USDC on Arc Mainnet

Because USDC is the native gas token on Arc Network, you need a small amount of USDC in your wallet to pay transaction fees:

* **Bridge from another chain:** Open **Cross Pay** (`/cross-pay`) to bridge USDC from Ethereum, Base, Arbitrum, OP Mainnet, Polygon, or Avalanche using Circle CCTP.
* **Receive from another wallet:** Share your address or QR code from **Receive** (`/receive`).
* **Testnet only:** If you are testing on Arc Testnet, use the **Faucet** button in the top bar (powered by Circle's testnet faucet).

## 4. Claim your `@username`

1. Open **Settings** (`/settings`) from the navigation menu.
2. Enter your desired username (3 to 30 characters: lowercase letters `a–z`, numbers `0–9`, and `_`).
3. Click **Register Username (Free)** and approve the transaction in your wallet. Registration has no EasyZpay protocol fee; you only pay standard Arc network gas in USDC.

## 5. Send your first payment

1. Open **Send** (`/send`).
2. Enter a recipient `@username` or `0x` address, type the USDC amount, and optionally add a memo.
3. Review the recipient address and click **Send** to approve and broadcast the transfer.
