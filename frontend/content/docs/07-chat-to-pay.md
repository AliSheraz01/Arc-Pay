The **Chat to Pay** page (`/chat-to-pay`) is where you link your EasyZpay wallet to messaging bots and manage your active chat session.

## What is live today

* **Telegram Bot (`@EasyzpayBot`):** Active. You can pair your Telegram account with a spending cap and expiry window, then send USDC by `@username` or `0x` address directly inside Telegram. See the [Telegram bot guide](/docs/telegram-bot) for full steps.

## Coming soon

* **X (Twitter) mention and DM bot:** Sending USDC by mentioning or DMing an automated bot on X is **not live yet**. Currently, X handles are only supported as a recipient lookup type inside the web **Send** page when the recipient has connected their X account in EasyZpay.

> [!NOTE]
> You can disconnect or revoke your Telegram pairing at any time from `/chat-to-pay` in the web app or by sending `/disconnect` inside Telegram.
